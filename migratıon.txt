====================================================

PROJECT OVERVIEW

================



Project Name:

DischargeGuardian AI



Hackathon Context:

Agents Assemble healthcare AI hackathon.



Core Idea:

DischargeGuardian AI helps hospitals reduce preventable readmissions after discharge.



The system takes complex discharge summaries and patient context, then generates:



1. Clinician-facing technical discharge summary

2. Patient-friendly home care plan

3. Critical safety alerts

4. Medication risk explanations

5. Follow-up reminders

6. Low health literacy adapted instructions



This is not a generic chatbot.

This is a structured healthcare discharge transition workflow product.



Main patient scenario:

A 72-year-old female heart failure patient is discharged after volume overload admission.

She has:



* HFrEF, LVEF 35%

* CKD stage 3

* Type 2 diabetes

* Hypertension

* Hyperlipidemia

* Furosemide dose increased from 40mg BID to 80mg BID

* Borderline low potassium: K+ 3.8

* BNP 450

* Creatinine 1.2

* Fall risk

* Medication confusion risk

* Low health literacy

* Lives alone

* Needs electrolyte panel in 72 hours

* Cardiology follow-up in 7 days

* Daily weight monitoring



The product should show how AI converts a complex discharge note into safe, understandable, actionable discharge guidance.



Core slogan:

“Doctors write discharge summaries for clinicians. DischargeGuardian rewrites them for patients.”
